Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of how Senior Post handles information when you use our app and website.

1. Overview

Senior Post (“Senior Post,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Senior Post mobile application and this website (together, the “Service”). Senior Post is a postal-style social service designed for adults aged 45 and over. It lets people write delayed letters, meet potential pen pals, maintain a mailbox, and schedule letters to their future selves.

This Policy describes the information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we keep it, and the choices available to you. It applies to the Senior Post Android application with package name cn.nine.pros.post.senior_post_flutter and any Senior Post website that links here.

Important: Letters are user-generated content. Do not include highly sensitive information that you would not want a recipient, safety reviewer, or service provider involved in delivery and moderation to process.

2. Data we collect

Account and authenticationEmail address, password hash, verification and password-reset records, Google account identifier if you choose Google Sign-In, account status, sign-up method, and login timestamps.
Profile and matchingNickname, avatar, birth year, gender if provided, country, city, biography, interests, language, writing-style preferences, and approximate or precise coordinates if you choose location access.
Letters and social activityLetter text, topic, paper and presentation choices, drafts, scheduled future letters, attachments you choose, sender and recipient details, delivery and read status, favorites, pen-pal requests and relationships, blocks, reports, and moderation results.
Device and technical dataDevice identifier generated for the app, device type, push-notification token, IP address, user agent, app version, language, login and security records, service actions, and error information.
Preferences and transactionsNotification choices, delivery preferences, feature entitlements, subscription or purchase status if paid features are offered, and related transaction references. Payment card details are handled by the applicable app store or payment provider, not stored by Senior Post.
Support and safetyFeedback, support messages, report reasons, review notes, and information needed to investigate abuse, fraud, security incidents, or policy violations.

Information from other sources

If you use Google Sign-In, Google provides the account identifier and basic profile information that you authorize. We may derive an approximate country or city from an IP address, or receive purchase status from Google Play. Information is also generated when another user sends you a letter, reports content, or interacts with you.

3. Device permissions

  • Location: requested in context to help set your location, improve matching, and estimate a letter’s journey. You can deny access and enter supported location information manually. Senior Post does not request background location.
  • Photos and media: requested only when you choose an avatar or supported image attachment. On supported Android versions, the system photo picker may be used instead of broad library access.
  • Notifications: used to notify you about letter and account activity. You can disable notifications in the app or device settings.
  • Internet: required to sign in, send and receive letters, upload chosen media, and use online features.

Permissions can be changed in Android settings. Turning one off may disable the related feature but should not prevent unrelated features from working.

4. How we use data

  • create and secure your account, authenticate sign-ins, and provide customer support;
  • display your profile and preferences to support discovery, matching, and pen-pal relationships;
  • send, store, schedule, route, and deliver letters and attachments;
  • calculate distance-based delivery timing and show in-transit progress;
  • provide optional AI writing assistance at your request;
  • moderate content, respond to reports, enforce blocks and community rules, and protect users;
  • send essential service email and push notifications, and apply your notification choices;
  • diagnose errors, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and improve reliability;
  • administer entitlements or purchases where available; and
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

Where law requires a legal basis, we process data to perform our contract with you, based on consent where requested (for example, device permissions), for legitimate interests such as service security and improvement, or to comply with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent, but that does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

5. What other people can see

Your nickname, avatar, approximate location such as country or city, age or birth-year-derived age, biography, interests, and selected preferences may be shown to other users for discovery and matching. A letter and its supported attachments are visible to its sender and intended recipient after delivery, and may be available to authorized safety reviewers when necessary.

Letters to your future self are not intentionally shared with other users. They may still be processed by our infrastructure and safety systems to operate and protect the Service. Do not publish another person’s private information without permission.

6. AI assistance and content moderation

Senior Post may use automated tools and human review to detect spam, harassment, sexual exploitation, threats, illegal content, and other violations. Text may be processed by a configured AI service such as DeepSeek, and images may be processed by a configured moderation service such as Baidu. Automated results may be reviewed or overridden by authorized personnel.

When you deliberately open the writing assistant, the text you submit and the language of the interface are sent to the configured AI provider to generate a suggestion. AI output can be inaccurate or inappropriate. You decide whether to use, edit, or discard it and remain responsible for anything you send.

We do not permit providers to use Senior Post content for their own advertising. Provider handling is governed by our contracts and their applicable privacy terms. Avoid entering health, financial, government identification, account credentials, or other highly sensitive information into AI-assisted drafts.

7. When we disclose data

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose the minimum information necessary to:

  • Infrastructure providers: cloud hosting, PostgreSQL/Redis hosting, email delivery, and Alibaba Cloud OSS or another configured storage provider for chosen uploads;
  • Authentication and messaging providers: Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging when those features are enabled;
  • AI and moderation providers: configured text and image services used for requested assistance and safety review;
  • Other users: profile information and letters as described above;
  • Safety and legal recipients: law enforcement, regulators, courts, advisers, or affected people where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or investigate abuse; and
  • Business successors: a buyer or successor in a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice obligations.

Service providers may process data only to deliver contracted services or comply with law. The specific provider may change as our infrastructure evolves; material changes will be reflected in this Policy.

8. Retention

We keep account and profile data while your account is active. Letters, drafts, relationship records, preferences, reports, security logs, and support records are retained for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain mailbox history, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

When an account-deletion request becomes final, we delete or irreversibly de-identify data associated with the account, unless limited retention is necessary for fraud prevention, safety, legal claims, accounting, or another legal obligation. Data retained for those purposes is isolated, access-restricted, and removed when the retention reason ends. Backups may persist for a limited cycle before being overwritten. Content already received by another user may remain in that user’s mailbox with sender details removed or minimized where feasible.

9. Delete your account and data

You can start deletion in the app: Profile → Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account. Senior Post currently provides a seven-day cooling-off period. Signing in during that period may cancel the request. After the period ends, the account is closed and associated data enters the deletion or de-identification process described above.

Request deletion without the app

Email privacy@seniorpost.app from the address linked to your account with the subject “Senior Post account deletion request.” Include your nickname only if needed to locate the account. We may ask you to verify ownership; we will never ask for your password.

Email a deletion request

You may separately ask us to delete specific information without deleting the entire account. Some information may need to be retained for the reasons in Section 8. Removing the app from your device does not delete your account.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, download, restrict, object to, or delete personal data, or to appeal a decision. You can edit many profile details and notification settings in the app. You may also contact us to exercise a right or withdraw consent.

We will verify requests and respond within the period required by applicable law. You may complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

11. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including access controls, password hashing, signed upload/download links, token-based sessions, and transport encryption where supported. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your sign-in details and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

12. International processing

Senior Post supports connections across countries. Data may be processed in countries other than yours by us and our providers. Where required, we use contractual, organizational, or other recognized safeguards for international transfers. Local law in a processing country may differ from the law where you live.

13. Age limits

Senior Post is designed for adults aged 45 and over and is not directed to children. You must be at least 45 to create an account. If we learn that a child has provided personal data, we will take appropriate steps to remove the account and data. Contact us if you believe this has occurred.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service, providers, or law changes. We will post the revised version here with a new effective date and provide additional notice in the app or by email when a change is material and required by law.

15. Contact us

Senior Post is the operator responsible for this Service. For privacy questions, rights requests, or complaints, email privacy@seniorpost.app. For general support, email support@seniorpost.app.
