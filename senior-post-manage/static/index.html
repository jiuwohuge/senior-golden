Questions

A few things to know.

Is Senior Post a dating app?

No. Senior Post is a letter-based social space for friendship and thoughtful conversation. Romantic or sexual solicitation is not the purpose of the service.

Why do letters take time to arrive?

The wait creates room to reflect and removes the expectation of constant availability. Delivery estimates are shown before and after sending.

Do I have to use AI writing help?

No. It is optional. If you use it, your draft is processed to generate suggestions, and you remain responsible for reviewing the final letter.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Request deletion in the app under Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account, or use the web instructions in our Privacy Policy.

Who is Senior Post for?

The service is designed for adults aged 45 and over. It is not directed to children.
