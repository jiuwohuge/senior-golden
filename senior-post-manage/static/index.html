Our Features

SP

Four thoughtfully crafted postal experiences, designed for the pace and wisdom of life after 45.

Post Wall

Share postcards with the world. Write your thoughts, attach a photo, and let your words travel to strangers who become friends.

Post Directory

Browse pen pals in a beautiful letter-rack style grid. Filter by country, interests, or language and start a correspondence.

Post Box

Send Registered Mail for instant delivery, or choose Standard Post — letters that arrive in 10 minutes to 2 hours, just like the real thing.

Chat Stamps

Earn stamps through daily activity and spend them to send letters. A gentle, meaningful economy that makes every message count.

How It Works

Getting started is as simple as addressing an envelope.

1

Create Your Profile

2

3

Write & Correspond

Slow Social for the Wise Generation

SP

Senior Post was built on a simple belief: the best conversations take time. In a world of instant reactions and endless scrolling, we offer something different — a place where words are chosen carefully, friendships grow slowly, and every letter matters.

This is not a dating app. There is no matching algorithm, no swiping, no romantic intent. Senior Post is purely about friendship, curiosity, and the timeless joy of correspondence with people who share your season of life.
